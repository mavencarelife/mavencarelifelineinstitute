Common questions

What would you like to know?

What courses are offered?

We offer practical, care-focused, and business-related courses. View the full course catalog for current titles, durations, and fees.

How do I find course fees and duration?

Durations and fees are listed in the course catalog. Confirm the current details with admissions before enrollment.

How do I apply or ask about entry requirements?

Choose a course, then contact the institute for entry requirements and application guidance. Confirm intake details and fees before finalising. See the admissions page for next steps.

Where is the institute located?

The institute is located at Along Busia-Kisumu Road, Tanaka Junction, Opposite Pramukh, Busia County, Kenya.

How do I contact admissions?

Visit the contact page to prepare an inquiry. Phone and WhatsApp details are awaiting confirmation, and the message builder does not send messages.
